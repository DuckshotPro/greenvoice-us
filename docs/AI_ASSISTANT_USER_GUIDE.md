# AI Assistant User Guide

## Overview

The AI Assistant is your intelligent business companion for invoice management. It provides expert advice on invoice creation, payment collection strategies, business insights, and general business questions. The assistant understands your specific needs and provides contextual, actionable advice.

## Getting Started

### Accessing the AI Assistant

1. **Navigation**: Click on the "AI Assistant" tab in the main navigation menu (look for the Bot icon)
2. **Direct Access**: Visit `/ai-assistant` in your browser
3. **Features Overview**: The main page shows available features and use cases

### Account Types and Usage Limits

Your usage limits depend on your account type:

| Account Type | Daily Interactions | Monthly Interactions |
|--------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Guest Account** | 3 | 10 |
| **Premium Account** | 100 | 1,000 |

**Usage Tracking**: Your remaining interactions are displayed in real-time at the top of the chat interface.

## Key Features

### 1. Invoice Content Generation

Ask the AI to help create professional invoice descriptions, terms, and conditions.

**Example Questions:**
- "Help me write a professional description for web development services"
- "What should I include in payment terms for a $5,000 consulting project?"
- "Create invoice line items for a complete website redesign"

**Sample Response:**
> "For web development services, I recommend this professional description: 'Custom website development including responsive design, content management system integration, SEO optimization, and cross-browser compatibility testing.' For payment terms on a $5,000 project, consider: 'Net 30 days from invoice date. Late payments subject to 1.5% monthly service charge.'"

### 2. Payment Collection Strategies

Get expert advice on collecting overdue payments professionally and effectively.

**Example Questions:**
- "How should I follow up on a 30-day overdue $1,500 invoice?"
- "What's the best approach for collecting from a long-term client?"
- "Help me write a professional payment reminder email"

**Sample Response:**
> "For a 30-day overdue $1,500 invoice with a long-term client, I recommend a gentle but firm approach: Start with a friendly reminder email highlighting the value you've provided. Offer a brief payment plan if needed. Maintain the relationship while clearly communicating payment expectations. Would you like me to draft a specific email template?"

### 3. Business Insights and Analytics

Analyze your invoice data and get recommendations for business improvement.

**Example Questions:**
- "My average payment time is 25 days. How can I improve this?"
- "What's a good strategy for handling seasonal cash flow variations?"
- "How often should I send invoice reminders?"

**Sample Response:**
> "A 25-day average payment time is reasonable but can be improved. Consider offering 2% early payment discounts for payments within 10 days, implementing automated reminder systems, and clearly stating payment terms upfront. For seasonal businesses, build cash reserves during peak periods and consider invoice factoring during slow seasons."

### 4. General Business Consultation

Ask about business best practices, client management, and growth strategies.

**Example Questions:**
- "How should I structure my pricing for consulting services?"
- "What's the best way to handle scope creep in projects?"
- "Should I require deposits for large projects?"

## How to Use the Chat Interface

### Starting a Conversation

1. **Type Your Question**: Enter your question in the chat input field
2. **Be Specific**: Include relevant details like amounts, timelines, or client types
3. **Send Message**: Click send or press Enter
4. **Wait for Response**: The AI will provide detailed, contextual advice

### Chat Features

- **Message History**: Previous conversations are saved during your session
- **Real-time Responses**: Messages appear as they're generated
- **Usage Counter**: See your remaining daily/monthly interactions
- **Error Handling**: Clear messages if you reach usage limits

### Best Practices for Questions

#### ✅ Good Questions
- "How do I handle a client who hasn't paid a $2,000 invoice that's 45 days overdue?"
- "What payment terms should I use for a 6-month project worth $15,000?"
- "Help me create line items for social media management services"

#### ❌ Less Effective Questions
- "Help me with invoices" (too vague)
- "What should I do?" (lacks context)
- "Money problems" (unclear request)

## Understanding Usage Limits

### Daily and Monthly Limits

- **Daily Limits**: Reset at midnight (local time)
- **Monthly Limits**: Reset on the 1st of each month
- **Tracking**: Usage is tracked per conversation, not per message

### When You Reach Limits

If you exceed your usage limits, you'll see:

1. **Limit Warning**: When approaching your limit
2. **Limit Reached**: Clear message explaining the restriction
3. **Reset Time**: When your usage will reset
4. **Upgrade Option**: How to get more interactions (premium accounts)

### Optimizing Your Usage

- **Plan Your Questions**: Think about what you most need help with
- **Be Specific**: Get better answers with detailed questions
- **Combine Topics**: Ask about multiple related issues in one conversation
- **Save Responses**: Copy important advice for future reference

## Error Messages and Solutions

### Common Error Messages

1. **"Daily usage limit reached"**
   - **Solution**: Wait until tomorrow or upgrade to premium
   - **Reset Time**: Shown in the error message

2. **"Monthly usage limit reached"**
   - **Solution**: Wait until next month or upgrade to premium
   - **Reset Time**: 1st of next month

3. **"Message too long"**
   - **Solution**: Break your question into smaller parts
   - **Limit**: Keep messages under 1,000 characters

4. **"Service temporarily unavailable"**
   - **Solution**: Try again in a few minutes
   - **Cause**: Usually temporary API issues

### Getting Help

If you experience issues:

1. **Check Usage**: Verify your remaining interactions
2. **Refresh Page**: Sometimes resolves temporary issues
3. **Try Shorter Messages**: If you get length errors
4. **Contact Support**: For persistent technical issues

## Tips for Maximum Value

### 3. Apply Industry Context

- "As a graphic designer, how should I handle..."
- "For consulting services, what's the best way to..."
- "In the marketing industry, how do you typically..."

### 4. Use for Planning

- "What invoicing changes should I make for next quarter?"
- "How do I prepare for seasonal payment delays?"
- "What systems should I implement as my business grows?"

## Privacy and Security

- **No Data Storage**: Conversations are not permanently stored
- **Session-Based**: Chat history clears when you close the browser
- **Anonymous**: Guest users are tracked by session, not personal data
- **Secure**: All communications are encrypted and secure

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can the AI access my actual invoice data?**
A: No, the AI Assistant doesn't access your stored invoices. You provide context in your questions.

**Q: Are my conversations private?**
A: Yes, conversations are session-based and not stored permanently.

**Q: Can I get more interactions per day?**
A: Yes, upgrade to a premium account for significantly higher limits.

**Q: What if I need help outside business hours?**
A: The AI Assistant is available 24/7, subject to your usage limits.

**Q: Can the AI create actual invoices for me?**
A: The AI provides guidance and suggestions. You'll create actual invoices using the platform's invoice creation tools.

**Q: How accurate is the business advice?**
A: The AI provides general best practices and suggestions. Always consider your specific situation and consult professionals for legal or complex financial advice.
